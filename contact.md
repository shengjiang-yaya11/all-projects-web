## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### For New Customers

1. **Send Inquiry** - Email us your requirements
2. **Receive Quote** - Detailed pricing within 24 hours
3. **Confirm Sample** - Review prototype before mass production
4. **Place Order** - Production begins after approval
5. **Receive Products** - Secure shipping worldwide

### For POD (Print-on-Demand) Customers

POD service is perfect for:
- Startups testing new products
- Dropshippers needing custom branding
- Small businesses with low MOQ requirements
- Corporate gift customization

**POD Process:**
1. Choose product from our catalog
2. Send your logo/design files (AI, PDF, or high-res PNG)
3. Approve digital mockup within 48 hours
4. Production: 7-10 days for 50-500 units
5. Quality inspection and shipping

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is your MOQ?**
A: Standard products: 100 units. POD service: 50 units. OEM/ODM: Depends on product complexity.

**Q: How long does production take?**
A: Samples: 5-7 days. Mass production: 15-30 days depending on quantity and complexity.

**Q: Do you provide samples?**
A: Yes, we provide samples for quality verification before mass production. Sample cost is refundable upon bulk order.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: T/T, PayPal, Western Union, Alipay, WeChat Pay. For new customers, we accept 30% deposit + 70% before shipping.

**Q: Can you help with certification?**
A: Yes, we provide CE, FCC, RoHS, UL, KC, PSE certification support and documentation.

**Q: Do you offer dropshipping?**
A: Yes, our POD service supports direct shipping to your customers with your branding.
