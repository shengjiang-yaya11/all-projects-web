- **PCB Fabrication** - FR4, aluminum, flexible PCB options
- **Quick-turn PCB** - 24-hour prototype service available

- **Mechanical Engineering** - Enclosure design, thermal analysis

### Firmware Development

- **IoT Solutions** - WiFi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRa
- **Smart Controls** - Touch panels, displays, sensors
- **OTA Updates** - Over-the-air firmware update systems
